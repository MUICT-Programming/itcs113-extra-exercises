# X: XBox

Poon, a gamer, is curious about the Xbox logo. He wonders why it's an X in a circle instead of a square box. To create a new and accurate Xbox logo, he kindly requests you to write it in C. Firstly, it should have an X (obviously). Then have a square box cover the X.

> Every `*` is `"* "` — for the last one of each row, it requires `" "` after the `"*"`. Otherwise your submission may not be accepted.

## Input Format

An integer defining the width and height of the box with X inside.

## Constraints

`3 < n`

## Output Format

A beautiful new XBox Logo.

## Sample Input 0

```
16
```

## Sample Output 0

```
* * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * 
*  *                        *  * 
*    *                    *    * 
*      *                *      * 
*        *            *        * 
*          *        *          * 
*            *    *            * 
*              *  *            * 
*              *  *            * 
*            *    *            * 
*          *        *          * 
*        *            *        * 
*      *                *      * 
*    *                    *    * 
*  *                        *  * 
* * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * 
```
